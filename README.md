# NER-ResQRoute 🚨

### Disaster-Aware Logistics Intelligence for the North Eastern Region

NER-ResQRoute is a disaster-aware logistics and route intelligence platform designed to help logistics teams navigate complex terrain, changing weather conditions, road blockages, and field-level disruptions across the North Eastern Region.

The platform combines GIS data, GPS/vehicle tracking, weather-risk signals, field reports, and mission priorities to continuously assess road accessibility and recommend safer, more practical routes.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Logistics operations in the North Eastern Region face challenges such as:

- Difficult and mountainous terrain
- Landslides, floods, and weather-related disruptions
- Limited visibility into road accessibility
- Delayed field-level reporting
- Static routing that cannot adapt quickly to incidents
- Difficulty prioritizing essential deliveries
- Poor connectivity in remote areas

A route that is technically available may become unsafe or inaccessible minutes later.

NER-ResQRoute addresses this by creating a continuously updated **road accessibility and mission intelligence layer**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

NER-ResQRoute creates a dynamic logistics decision system that combines:

**GIS + GPS + Weather Risk + Field Intelligence + Mission Priority**

to calculate accessibility, identify risks, trigger alerts, and support dynamic route decisions.

### Core capabilities

- 🗺️ **Live GIS Terrain Mapping**
- 🚚 **Real-Time Vehicle Tracking**
- 🛣️ **Dynamic Route Orchestration**
- ⚠️ **Automated Alert Ecosystem**
- 👷 **On-Ground Field Reporting**
- 📦 **Mission-Aware Logistics**
- ☁️ **Offline Sync & Reporting**
- 📊 **Command Center Dashboard**
